# Synopsis

Written from the script (2026-10-30) for translators and story reviewers. Ids are `msg36:NNNN` as `36:NNNN`. Script
order is only roughly story order. "(inferred)" = not stated outright.

## Cast
- **Reem Likeburn**: male hero, "Knight of Lightning", leads the Heroes for King Richard; Tamtam calls him "our master". Soft-spoken (ボク/僕), formal keigo with royals. *Heroes, leader.*
- **Moomoo**: male minotaur, Reem's best friend (Yago calls him a monster, 37:0326). Rough and casual (オレさま, ねえ), now and then ends a line with モー. Glutton, a bit dense. *Heroes, muscle.*
- **Tamtam**: female priest, Radock's former pupil, reads Calard script. Warm, feminine わ/よ. Jealous of Lime (inferred, 37:0177-0183). *Heroes, healer.*
- **Charles Bread**: female amber witch and Matedral priest, Tamtam's best friend. Brash, あたし, "ムッキー". A shock flips her into Lilulu. *Heroes, Matedral liaison.*
- **Lilulu**: Charles's gentle second personality, support magic. Polite です/ます with drawn-out ～ぅ, calls Reem 勇者様. *Heroes.*
- **Weiss Campel**: male tomb-raider boss, Peacock's "boss"; dreams of a nation where the low-born are equal. Rough べらんめえ (俺, ぜ, ねえ). *Rival, later redeemed.*
- **Yago**: Weiss's cat "partner". Childish ボク, ニャ/ニャン endings. *Hidden villain.*
- **Sougetsu**: Skull Bombers' strategist (Moomoo calls him "WolfCommand", 36:0335; male, inferred). Polite, ironic keigo, 私. *Bombers, then reluctant ally.*
- **Trump**: male Bombers thug boss. 俺様, ヒャッハッハ, calls Reem チビスケ ("shorty"). *Bombers, brute.*
- **Dr. Skull**: male Bombers trap-builder, runs the Tower of Solomon. 我輩, signs off ヒャハ♡. *Bombers, gimmick boss.*
- **Professor Radock**: male mouse sage (37:0158, ev0A:0270), mentor of Tamtam and Charles. Old-man じゃ/ぞい/のお, rambles (くどくど). *Heroes, lore source.*
- **Peacock**: male, Radock's aide (ev22:0009) and Weiss's underling. Timid, っス, あっし/おいら. *Weiss's band, loyal.*
- **Richard III**: King of Rainark, Hauser's old friend. Regal archaic じゃ/おる. *Rainark.*
- **Lime**: Princess of Rainark (Richard's daughter, inferred), has a crush on Reem. Shy, hesitant ‥, polite. *Rainark.*
- **Hauser**: male Pontiff of Matedral, pious, against all fighting. Archaic 余/じゃ. *Matedral, bewitched.*
- Civilians: **Borscht** (rustic だぁよ) and son **Potofu** (Kurukuru); **Misha**, **Misha's mom**, **Poo** (Misha's plush toy, Ausam); **Host** (Quiz Village, ですぞ); **Tappun** (rustic merchant); **Hamus** (hamster, battle cries only, 36:0080-0082).

## Plot by arc
1. **Prologue** (36:0126-0135): Kingdom Year 137, Jasvan, a ニャ-speaking creature is sealed. Present day: Weiss and Trump rally their gangs.
2. **Happle Ruins / Pazuta** (36:0136-0210; ev22:0083-0097; ev0A:0013-0030): the party rescues Radock and Peacock after a quake traps them. Radock's document: "In Jasvan, wishes come true." Trump's Bombers raid Pazuta and take "that stone" Radock asked for.
3. **Rainark, Weiss on the road** (36:0211-0267): Hauser asked Richard for help against the Bombers and tomb raiders. Lime frets over Reem. Weiss invites Reem into his nation-building, then heads for Jasvan.
4. **Matedral and Jasvan** (36:0268-0350, 0382-0426; 37:0001-0005): Hauser sends Charles as guide. Sougetsu and Trump appear. Weiss's band loots the Jasvan altar (ev0A:0042-0065); a fleeing raider drops a "pebble" that Moomoo pockets (ev0A:0055-0058). The scraped tablet goes to Radock: the treasure was already taken.
5. **Weiss commands Matedral** (36:0427-0504): Weiss appears with Yago and explains the 7 Rainbow Jewels. Hauser: "any sacrifice is acceptable." Lilulu joins. Green is in Matedral, Yellow in a dry land, Orange in Kurukuru, Indigo is the Quiz Village crown, Blue and Violet unknown, Red is Lime's headband.
6. **Jirijiri Desert** (36:0505-0545): the altar is empty (Weiss took Yellow, inferred). Cutaway 0528-0536: "????" (Weiss) and "??" (Yago) offer the Bombers a deal. 0507-0514 repeats 0340-0350.
7. **Kurukuru, Toy Tower, Ausam, Quiz Village** (36:0547-0623; ev0A:0124-0177, 0253-0258): a Barbar Bird feather cures Borscht's son and earns the **Orange** Jewel (Borscht's "pebble") and a book with a shape in it. The party frees the children Trump holds for ransom, gets Poo back from a snowman, and wins the **Indigo** Crown of Wisdom. (36:0604 JP says オレンジ for the Quiz Village Jewel: a source error.)
8. **Radock kidnapped, Tower of Solomon** (36:0626-0637; 37:0006-0077; ev0A:0179-0274): Yago catches Radock at Jasvan. Reem trades **Orange and Indigo** for him. Sougetsu knows of Red, and the trade was a ploy. Radock, freed, gives Reem the **Blue** Jewel: Moomoo's pebble.
9. **Betrayals** (37:0079-0134; ev0A:0283-0302): Weiss now commands the Bombers. Sougetsu kidnaps Lime and takes **Red**. Weiss seizes **Green** and drops Hauser. Yago: "That's 5 Jewels."
10. **Kurukuru fire, Laimos** (37:0138-0185; ev0A:0306-0323, 0275-0282): Trump burns the forest, and the first rainbow appears. Sougetsu reveals where Lime is held and explains the Rainbow Road. Lime is rescued.
11. **Sougetsu duel** (37:0192-0232): monsters are a minority with no place of their own. Trump betrays Sougetsu, and Yago discards him. He gives Reem **Red** (inferred, 0223).
12. **Gaudi church finale** (37:0239-0266, 0314-0334, 0343-0362; ev0A:0330-0399; ev22:0027-0040, 0079-0081; ev03:0037-0047): Yago drops Weiss, who throws Reem his Jewels. In the demon-world passage the Jewels are set in rainbow order, and the Rainbow Road banishes Yago.
13. **Epilogue, post-game** (37:0274-0305, alt 0388-0415; 0312, 0342; ev0A:0401-0418): a wishes feast. Weiss atones by rebuilding Matedral. A cavern near Matedral Castle leads to the Mirage towers.

## Reveals and twists (don't tip these off early)
- **The sealed creature is Yago:** said at 37:0064 and 37:0254 (the pot). Keep the "??" labels (36:0535, 0627) and don't name him earlier.
- **Weiss took the Jasvan "treasure":** hinted at ev0A:0042-0065 and 36:0529-0534 ("????"); he openly turns at 37:0079-0084.
- **The treasure is the Rainbow Jewels**, and one Jewel can open the way: 37:0057-0068.
- **Hauser is bewitched:** hinted at 36:0470, confirmed at 37:0128.
- **The Rainbow Road banishes demons:** 37:0161-0163. Before that the rainbow is only "a bridge to the demon world" (36:0488).
- **Sougetsu secretly works to seal Yago:** 37:0151-0165. Trump betrays him at 37:0216.
