Little Master: Nijiiro no Maseki (Little Master: Jewels of Rainbow)
English translation patch v{version}

HOW TO PATCH
1. Get your own copy of the Japanese ROM:
     Little Master - Nijiiro no Maseki (Japan).sfc
     Size {size} bytes, no header
     CRC32 {crc32}
     MD5   {md5}
     SHA1  {sha1}
   If your file is 2,097,664 bytes, it has a 512-byte header: remove it first
   (e.g. with a header remover tool, or tick "remove header" in Rom Patcher JS).
2. Apply LittleMaster_EN_v{version}.bps with Floating IPS (Flips), beat,
   or Rom Patcher JS (https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/).
3. The patched ROM is 4 MB, CRC32 {out_crc32}.

Tested in Mesen2. Report problems with a screenshot and where they happened.
